## 👁️ synedrix-os — Technical Review identity of AI-WORKFLOWS.DEV

This account is the **Technical Reviewer** role of [AI-WORKFLOWS.DEV](https://github.com/AI-WORKFLOWS-DEV), operated by GP.

It is the same person as [@aiworkflowsdev](https://github.com/aiworkflowsdev), wearing a different hat, with different permissions.

### What this account does
- ✅ Reviews pull requests to `main`
- ✅ Requests changes or approves, with a structured review record
- ✅ Signs off only after automated evidence (tests, scans, AI review, plan-governance checks) is green

### What this account never does
- ❌ Author commits to AI-WORKFLOWS.DEV repositories (the only content this account publishes itself is this profile page)
- ❌ Change repository rules or settings
- ❌ Approve a PR within 4 hours of its last push (a review-discipline convention)

### Why
Solo practice, team-grade process. Separating the **author** and **reviewer** roles into distinct, permission-limited identities makes every decision visible and auditable, and makes it impossible to skip review under pressure.

How it works and its limits are documented in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/AI-WORKFLOWS-DEV/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
